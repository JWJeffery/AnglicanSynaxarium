Today the Church observes the Eve of the Nativity of our Lord Jesus Christ. We come to the end of Advent and keep watch as the feast of his birth begins.

God had called Abraham from his homeland and promised that through his family all the families of the earth would receive a blessing. He had delivered Israel from slavery and made a covenant with his people. To David he had promised a kingdom that would endure.

Yet Israel knew exile, defeat, and the failure of kings. God's people carried his promises through generations in which their fulfillment remained unseen. The prophets spoke into that waiting. Isaiah proclaimed light for those who walked in darkness, a child given to the people, and a reign of justice and peace.

When the time came, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, to a virgin named Mary. She was pledged in marriage to Joseph, of the house of David. Gabriel greeted her as one upon whom God's favor rested. She would bear a son and name him Jesus. The Lord God would give him David's throne, and his kingdom would have no end.

Mary asked how this could happen, since she had not known a man. The angel told her that the Holy Spirit would come upon her and the power of the Most High would overshadow her. The child would be holy, the Son of God.

Mary placed herself at God's service and consented to his word.

She then went to visit her kinswoman Elizabeth. When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the child in her womb leaped for joy. Filled with the Holy Spirit, Elizabeth welcomed the mother of her Lord. Mary answered with praise. God had looked upon his lowly servant and remembered the mercy promised to Abraham and his descendants.

Joseph, too, had to receive a word he could not have discovered for himself. When he learned that Mary was with child, he intended to end their betrothal quietly. In a dream, an angel told him to take Mary as his wife. Her child was conceived by the Holy Spirit. Joseph was to name him Jesus, for he would save his people from their sins.

Joseph rose from sleep and obeyed. He took Mary into his home.

The promises entrusted to Israel had entered the life of this household. Mary bore the Son of God in her womb. Joseph received the mother and the child whom God had entrusted to his care.

On this evening the Church gathers to hear again what God has done. We bring our waiting into the company of those who trusted his promise. The Savior for whom we long is the one Mary carried and Joseph welcomed.

Advent has also taught us to look for his coming in glory. The child whose birth we celebrate is the Lord who will judge the living and the dead. Our preparation belongs to the whole of life: the wrong we must put right, the neighbor who needs our mercy, the prayer we have neglected, and the welcome we can offer now.

As the feast begins, may the Holy Spirit make us ready to receive Christ. With Mary, may we consent to his word. With Joseph, may we rise to do what he asks.

## Collect

O God, you have caused this holy night to shine with the brightness of the true Light: Grant that we, who have known the mystery of that Light on earth, may also enjoy him perfectly in heaven; where with you and the Holy Spirit he lives and reigns, one God, in glory everlasting. Amen.
